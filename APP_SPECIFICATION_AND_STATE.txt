1. APPLICATION OVERVIEW & DOMAIN MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pure Max Water Factory OS is an enterprise-grade industrial Enterprise Resource
Planning (ERP) and Operations Management System tailored for packaged sachet
water and bottled water production, distribution, fleet tracking, and financial
accounting.

Key Core Metrics & Calculation Logic:
- Outer Film Packaging Yield: 1 Outer Film Set produces exactly 50 Water Bundles.
  Formula: Total Bundles = (Outer Sets Purchased/Used * 50) - Remaining Loose Sachets.
- Packaging Roll Tracking: Weight in Kilograms (KG). Tracks roll load onto machines,
  depletion rate, and waste percentage.
- Net Profit Formula: Gross Sales - (Operating Expenses + Raw Material Purchases + Repairs + Fuel).
- Attendance & Daily Wage Matrix: Automated wage calculations based on role and
  location tier (Factory Plant vs Field Distribution).

2. ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC) MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The system supports strict tiered roles with runtime UI enforcement & route guards:

1. DEVELOPER (`developer`):
   - Full unrestricted root access across all modules.
   - Access to System Health, PostgreSQL Query Terminal (PgAdmin), Live Logs,
     Reset DB / Purge Mock Data, Custom Theme Editor, Error Diagnostics.

2. CEO / OWNER (`ceo`):
   - Executive dashboard, full financials, gross & net profits, revenue analytics.
   - Attendance reports, sales overview, production monitoring, audit logs.
   - Restricted from developer low-level system settings.

3. GENERAL MANAGER (`manager`):
   - Operations oversight: Production logging, Sales verification, Expense approvals,
     Attendance approvals, User role assignment, Fleet GPS tracking, Inventory control.

4. ASSISTANT / SECOND MANAGER (`second_manager`):
   - Operational delegate to Manager: Attendance approvals, Production logs,
     Stock checks, Fleet monitoring, Sales recording.

5. SALES PRODUCTION OFFICER / SALES MANAGER (`sales_manager`):
   - Dedicated Daily Sales Module (SalesDailyRecordsModule) with detailed credit/cash tracking.
   - Production bundle receipt verification, Customer debt collection, Sales summary reports.
   - Restricted from engineering raw equipment logs and developer settings.

6. PRODUCTION ENGINEER (`engineer`):
   - Production batch entry, Machine maintenance logs, Outer film & Roll inventory intake,
     Equipment inspection & water quality testing logs (TDS, pH, micron filters).
   - Restricted from financial profit margins and user management.

7. MACHINE OPERATOR (`operator`):
   - Line-level machine status reporting (Running, Idle, Breakdown, Reloading roll).
   - Bundle production counts, damaged bundle logging, roll reel change tracking.

8. TRICYCLE DELIVERY STAFF (`tricycle_staff`):
   - Field sales dispatch, mobile GPS broadcaster, customer cash collections,
     individual daily sales submissions, personal attendance check-in.
   - Hidden from global fleet map view (can broadcast own GPS location).

9. VAN DELIVERY STAFF (`van_staff`):
   - Bulk delivery routing, mobile GPS broadcaster, bulk wholesale sales receipts,
     personal attendance check-in.

10. GENERAL FACTORY STAFF (`staff`):
    - Personal attendance check-in/check-out, team chat & announcements, profile.

3. NAVIGATION & ROUTING ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary Navigation (`src/components/common/Sidebar.tsx` & `App.tsx`):
- `dashboard`: All roles (Role-adaptive widgets and action queues).
- `fleet_map` / `map`: Developer, CEO, Manager, Second Manager, Sales Manager, Operator, Engineer.
  * Hidden from field delivery staff (`tricycle_staff`, `van_staff`, `staff`).
- `sales`: Developer, CEO, Manager, Second Manager.
- `production`:
  * Developer, CEO, Manager, Second Manager, Engineer -> Full Production & Raw Materials.
  * Sales Manager -> Specialized Sales Daily Records & Distribution tracking.
- `attendance`: All roles (Staff view own status; Managers view approval queues).
- `expenses`: Developer, CEO, Manager, Second Manager.
- `repairs`: Developer, CEO, Manager, Second Manager, Engineer.
- `equipment`: Developer, CEO, Manager, Second Manager, Engineer.
- `chat`: All authenticated team members (Real-time channels, Direct Messages, WebRTC calls).
- `users`: Developer, Manager, Second Manager.
- `reports`: Developer, CEO, Manager, Second Manager, Sales Manager.
- `system`: Developer only.
- `pgadmin`: Developer only.
- `profile`: All authenticated users.

4. MODULE BREAKDOWN & FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. DASHBOARD (`DashboardModule.tsx`):
   - Real-time distinction between "TODAY'S SUMMARY (24-Hour Period)" and "LIFETIME METRICS (All-Time)".
   - Daily Sales, Daily Expenses, Daily Net Profit, and Daily Production Bundles computed with strict current-day filters.
   - Live Weather and Sierra Leone currency conversion widgets.
   - Fast action shortcuts according to logged-in user role.

B. PRODUCTION & RAW MATERIALS (`ProductionModule.tsx` & `MachineRollTracker.tsx`):
   - Outer Film Tracking: Sets bought vs Sets used -> remaining loose bags calculation.
   - Packaging Roll Tracking: Initial KG, current roll weight, net wastage.
   - Machine Line Status: Real-time visual tracking of 4 production lines with assigned operators.
   - Aggregated Recharts Bar Charts for Manager/Developer (Daily, Monthly, Yearly breakdowns).
   - Production Quality Control: Damaged bundles counter and batch scrap rate.

C. SALES & DAILY DISPATCH (`SalesModule.tsx` & `SalesDailyRecordsModule.tsx`):
   - Cash vs Credit vs Orange Money / Afrimoney mobile payments.
   - Debtors and credit management with payment reminder logging.
   - Daily dispatch reconciliation for tricycle & van routes.
   - Receipt generation and printable invoice summaries.

D. FLEET GPS TRACKING (`FleetMapModule.tsx`):

   - Live GPS broadcasting via HTML5 Geolocation API with Makeni bounds fallback.

E. TEAM COLLABORATION & WEBRTC CALLING (`ChatModule.tsx` & `WebRTCCallModal.tsx`):
   - Real-time Socket.io chat with Department channels and Direct 1-on-1 messaging.
   - Voice note audio recorder and instant audio player.

F. ATTENDANCE & PAYROLL (`AttendanceModule.tsx`):

G. EXPENSES & MAINTENANCE (`ExpensesModule.tsx` & `RepairsModule.tsx`):
